Multi-Omics Analysis of Acute Myeloid Leukemia Using MOFA

## Multi-Omics Analysis of Acute Myeloid Leukemia Using MOFA

* Integrated **DNA methylation, RNA-seq, and miRNA** datasets from **200 AML patient samples** obtained from TCGA/LinkedOmics.
* Applied **Multi-Omics Factor Analysis (MOFA)** in **R** to identify latent factors and major sources of variation across multiple omics modalities.
* Analyzed relationships between identified factors and clinical characteristics including **disease status, gender, and race**.
* Identified important molecular features using **feature-weight analysis** and interpreted factor-specific contributions across omics datasets.
* Developed **heatmaps, scatter plots, and factor visualizations** to explore multi-omics patterns and clinical associations.
* Proposed future integration of **mutation data** and construction of omics-specific biological networks.
  **Technologies:** R, MOFA, Multi-Omics Integration, Bioinformatics, DNA Methylation, RNA-seq, miRNA, TCGA/LinkedOmics, Data Visualization
